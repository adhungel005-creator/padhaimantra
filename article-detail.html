How to Check SEE Result: Complete SMS, IVR & Website Portal Guide

Share this guide:

The National Examination Board (NEB), Office of the Controller of Examinations, Sanothimi, Bhaktapur has officially published the list of authorized platforms through which students can access the Secondary Education Examination (SEE) results. The board said results for both regular and grade improvement exams will be available via SMS, IVR, and certified websites.

"Students are advised to keep their 8-digit SEE Symbol Number and Date of Birth ready before checking the grade sheet online."

1. Check SEE Result via SMS & IVR

S.N. Service Provider Service Type Short Code
1 Nepal Telecom SMS / IVR 1600
2 Janaki Technology SMS 35001
3 Sparrow SMS SMS 31003

2. Check SEE Result with Marksheet Online

To download your official electronic grade sheet, navigate to the official National Examination Board or Nepal Telecom website portals:

  • NEB Official Portal: www.neb.gov.np
  • SEE Controller Office: www.see.gov.np
  • Nepal Telecom Results: see.ntc.net.np
  • Kantipur Publications: www.ekantipur.com
