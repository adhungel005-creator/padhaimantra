1. Information Collected

We may collect:

  • Full name, email, and contact number
  • Login and authentication details
  • Course enrollment and payment records
  • Device and usage data for security and performance

2. Use of Information

User data is used strictly for:

  • Providing educational services and course access
  • Verifying enrollment and fee transactions
  • Academic and administrative communication
  • Platform security and service improvement

3. Data Protection and Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are applied to secure student information. Access is limited to authorized personnel only. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed, but industry best practices are followed.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may use limited user data for academic marketing and student benefit purposes. Disclosure only occurs if required by Nepalese law or lawful authority.

5. Privacy of Minors

The platform primarily serves students aged 13–17. Parental or guardian consent is assumed upon registration. Guardians may contact Padhai Mantra for privacy concerns.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

Padhai Mantra may revise these Terms and Privacy Policy as required. Continued use of the Platform implies acceptance of updated policies.
