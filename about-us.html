Empowering Nepal's Next Generation

Padhai Mantra is Nepal's dedicated ed-tech initiative designed to make high-quality SEE and Class 11–12 education accessible to every student, everywhere.

Our Vision

Quality Education for Every Student

To empower students across Nepal with the guidance and support they need to excel academically. We believe quality education should be accessible without compromising standards or leaving students to navigate complex curricula alone. At Padhai Mantra, we go beyond academics by helping students build critical thinking, problem-solving, and independent learning skills, preparing them for both exams and future success.

Our Philosophy

Beyond Rote Memorization

At Padhai Mantra, we believe education goes beyond exams. It is about building strong foundations by connecting academic knowledge, practical skills, and personal growth. Our focus is on helping students move beyond memorization to develop deep understanding, critical thinking, problem-solving, and effective communication. We aim to create confident learners who can apply their knowledge in real-life situations.

Leadership & Vision

Dr. Anurag Silwal

Founder & Director

With 6+ years of dedicated SEE teaching experience and a thriving YouTube community of 190K+ students, Anurag sir founded Padhai Mantra to revolutionize SEE preparation in Nepal. His student-focused approach and deep understanding of the curriculum have helped thousands of students achieve their dream scores!
