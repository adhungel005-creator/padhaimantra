Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find solutions to the common questions about Padhai Mantra batches and methodology.

Padhai Mantra is a dedicated Nepali ed-tech platform specifically designed for SEE (Class 10) and NEB Class 11–12 students. We deliver structured exam-oriented online courses, interactive live classes, mock tests, and mentor support to help learners achieve top GPA results.
Unlike generic tutorials, Padhai Mantra follows the exact curriculum prescribed by the National Examination Board (NEB) of Nepal. We combine live interactive problem solving, dedicated Discord community doubt clearing, and regular weekly mock exams with feedback.
All live interactive classes, video lecture recordings, and mock tests are hosted inside our web platform and official Padhai Mantra mobile app. Enrolled students also get access to our moderated Discord community for continuous discussions.
You get much more than live classes: complete recorded session archives for 24/7 revision, downloadable offline notes & PDFs, weekly MCQ test practices, and periodic masterclasses conducted by previous 4.0 GPA SEE and NEB toppers.
We align directly with students' academic milestones — whether securing entrance admission into top Kathmandu +2 colleges (St. Xavier's, KMC, SOS) or mastering board exam past papers through structured revision timelines.
Our faculty is led by experienced educators including Dr. Anurag Silwal (Founder & Director) and specialized subject mentors in Mathematics, Science, and English with extensive experience teaching Nepal's curriculum.
